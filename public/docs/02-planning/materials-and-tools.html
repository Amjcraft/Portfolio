Overview #

Quantified bill of materials (BOM) for the building shell and interior of the ~9 × 8 ft freestanding outdoor sauna, with rough cost ranges. Rolls up into budget.md.

Shopping? See Materials Summary — a consolidated buy-list with the exact wood specs (kiln-dried, grade, T&G profile, grain) to request.

Scope & accuracy: Preliminary takeoffs for the ~9 × 8 ft footprint (front/back walls widened to ~9 ft so the door fits beside the heater), 8 ft → ~9.5 ft shed-roof, raised 2×6 floor on the existing slab. Prices are rough 2026 PNW (Bend/Portland) ranges, cedar throughout. Electrical + heater deferred (separate section). Planning estimate, not a quote.

Design basis for the takeoff #

  • Footprint ~9.5 × 8 ft exterior; interior ~8.2 × 6.7 ft. Perimeter ~35 ft.
  • Walls: 2×6 @ 16″ OC → R-23 (upgraded from 2×4/R-15 for the cold climate).
  • Base: raised PT 2×6 floor on the existing slab; drain in the cavity → French drain.
  • Wall area ≈ 306 ft²; roof ≈ 88 ft²; ceiling ≈ ~55 ft².
  • Interior wall + ceiling to clad/insulate ≈ ~305 ft² (smaller than the 2×4 version — thicker walls shrink the interior, which reduces the cladding area).
  • Openings: DIY glazed door with a large lite (22″ leaf + ~3″ frame each side ≈ 28″ RO) beside heater. Rear window removed.
  • Benches: L-shaped, two-tier, back-wall legs stretched to the back wall, ~46 ft² seat.
  • Wood: Western Hemlock for walls/ceiling/duckboard; Western Red Cedar (clear VG) for bench slats; hem-fir for the (hidden) bench frame; exterior siding cheap non-cedar. See cost-options.html.

Shell + interior total (excludes electrical/heater) #

Low High
Sum of subtotals ~$5,788 ~$11,578
+ Contingency (~12%) ~$695 ~$1,389
Estimated total ~$6,500 ~$13,000

Cedar is only the benches — seat tops + the upper front apron (lower front left open). Hemlock walls/ceiling + no rear window keep the total down; the 2×6 upgrade is ~cost-neutral. (Permeable sheathing #3, if added, +$310–390.)

The remaining big swing is interior cedar grade. Now that siding is cheap non-cedar and the door is DIY, the low↔high spread is driven mostly by the interior cladding grade (knotty ~$6/ft² vs. clear VG ~$16/ft² on ~370 ft²). Choosing knotty or a knotty+clear mix pulls the total toward the low end.

Tools #

  • Layout: tape, level, speed + framing square, chalk line, string line
  • Cutting: circular saw, miter saw, jigsaw
  • Fastening: drill/driver, impact driver, brad/finish nailer (cladding)
  • Slab/platform anchoring: hammer drill + masonry bit (anchor bolts only)
  • Drain: PVC hand saw + deburring tool, PVC primer + solvent cement, shovel (French drain)
  • Safety: gloves, eye/ear protection, respirator (mineral wool + drilling dust)

Note: Mark tools owned vs. buy/rent. Renting a nailer and miter saw is often cheaper than buying for a one-off build.
