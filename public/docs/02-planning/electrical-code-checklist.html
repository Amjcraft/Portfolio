Overview #

Step-by-step code checks from the house main panel to the finished heater, for our run: 50 A breaker → #6/3 NM → garage j-box → 4 × #6 singles in conduit (~30 ft) → j-box → #6/3 NM in crawlspace (60–70 ft) → j-box (inside) → THWN-2 singles in exterior conduit → exterior subpanel (45 A heater, 15/20 A lights) → 8 kW heater.

Refs are NEC 2023; 2026: notes renumbering. OR = Oregon Table 1-E amendment; ORSC = Oregon residential code; Mfr = Harvia US manual (enforceable via 110.3(B)). The 2026 OESC (NEC 2026 + OR amendments) takes effect Oct 1, 2026.

Warning: Safety- and code-critical. Each section was reviewed against NEC 2023/2026, Oregon Table 1-E (2023 & 2026), ORSC M1902, and the Harvia US manuals (2026-09-27). This is a checklist, not a code substitute — confirm with City of Bend Building Safety (541-388-5580).

Open decisions (resolve before buying/building) #

  • [ ] Power unit location. Harvia: dry, not outdoors, outside the hot room, 32–104 °F. No anteroom → need a vestibule, a house/garage wall, or a built-in-control heater. Sensor lead is ~13 ft.
  • [ ] Heater circuit route. Across the header is the hottest zone — #8 fails above ~60 °C. Reroute low (4–6″ off floor).
  • [ ] Exhaust fan location. Bath fans are ~40 °C rated; Mfr: no fan in the sauna. Put it outside the hot room (inline/exterior).
  • [ ] Receptacle. Code requires one; Mfr forbids one inside. Get AHJ OK on an exterior one in writing.
  • [ ] Slab rebar. Ask AHJ whether the existing slab must be bonded as an electrode.
  • [ ] Room volume. ~408 ft³ is near the top of 8 kW ranges; KIP-80 max 425 ft³, Virta/Cilindro ceiling ≤ 7′6″.

0. Before starting #

  • [ ] Owner (a natural person, not an LLC) pulls the permit; owner or immediate family does the work (ORS 479.540, OAR 918-261-0040).
  • [ ] AHJ confirmed: City of Bend (in city) or Deschutes County.
  • [ ] Code edition confirmed (2023 vs 2026 OESC).
  • [ ] No building permit needed: ≤ 200 sq ft, ≤ 15 ft tall (ORSC R105.2). ORSC M1902 still applies.
  • [ ] Zoning setbacks checked (BDC 3.6.200).
  • [ ] Heater manual on hand (110.3(B)).
  • [ ] Heater breaker = 45 A: 33.3 A × 125% = 41.7 A (continuous load; 210.20(A), 422.10). Not 40 A.
  • [ ] One-line diagram + load calc ready (220.83; 2026: 120.83).
  • [ ] Call 811 before driving ground rods.

1. Main panel (50 A feeder breaker) #

  • [ ] 50 A 2-pole breaker listed for the panel (110.3(B)).
  • [ ] Panel bus/main has room for the added load.
  • [ ] No double-taps; one neutral per terminal (408.41).
  • [ ] NM secured with a listed clamp; one cable per clamp unless listed for two (312.5©; 2026: 312.8©).
  • [ ] Neutral to neutral bar, ground to ground bar (bonded here — it’s the service).
  • [ ] Lugs torqued to marked spec (110.14(D)).
  • [ ] Directory entry “Sauna subpanel” (408.4(A)).
  • [ ] Unused knockouts closed (110.12(A)).
  • [ ] Service barriers reinstalled if the panel has them (OR 230.62©).
  • [ ] Feeder check: ~41.7 A heater + ~2.5 A lights ≈ 44 A ≤ 50 A; #6 NM = 55 A (215.2, 334.80).

2. NM cable runs (panel → garage, crawlspace) #

  • [ ] #6/3 Cu NM-B with ground.
  • [ ] Listed staples/straps sized for 6/3; never stapled on edge (OR 334.2, 334.30).
  • [ ] Secured within 12″ of each box, every 4½ ft; ≤ 18″ from box to first support (OR 334.30).
  • [ ] Holes ≥ 1¼″ from framing edge, else 1/16″ steel plate; same for parallel runs (300.4(A), (D); 2026: 300.6).
  • [ ] Crawlspace: 6/3 may attach to joist bottoms only when crossing joists; parallel runs on joist sides (OR 334.15©).
  • [ ] Cable kept off the soil; crawlspace dry/vented (334.12(B)(4) — damp is AHJ’s call).
  • [ ] Garage: exposed NM run horizontally < 8 ft above floor protected by EMT/Sch 80/etc. (OR 334.15(B)).
  • [ ] Bend radius ≥ 5× cable diameter (334.24).
  • [ ] Holes through the garage–house wall firestopped (ORSC R302).
  • [ ] NM never in a wet location or outdoor box/conduit (334.12(B)(4)).

3. Junction boxes (all three transitions) #

  • [ ] House-exit box is inside the crawlspace — NM can’t enter an outdoor box; conduit leaves through the wall.
  • [ ] Accessible without removing finish (314.29).
  • [ ] Box ≥ 4-11/16″ square × 2-1/8″ deep (42 in³); 4×4 boxes fail (314.16: 5 in³ per #6).
  • [ ] Mounted to structure (314.23).
  • [ ] Insulated multi-tap (Polaris-type) connectors listed for #6 Cu, torqued (110.14(B), (D)).
  • [ ] No wire nuts unless listed for #6.
  • [ ] Listed connectors at every cable/conduit entry; covers on.
  • [ ] ≥ 6″ free conductor in each box (300.14).
  • [ ] All grounds spliced together; metal box bonded with a green screw (250.148).

4. Single-conductor conduit runs (garage + exterior) #

  • [ ] Exterior raceway: THWN-2 (wet-rated) conductors (310.10©; 300.9; 2026: 300.11(A)). THHN OK in the dry garage.
  • [ ] Neutral white/gray insulation — #6 can’t be re-marked (200.6(A)).
  • [ ] Ground green or bare — #6 can’t be re-marked (250.119(A)).
  • [ ] #6 EGC wire in every segment — PVC can’t serve as ground (250.118).
  • [ ] All four conductors in the same raceway (300.3(B)).
  • [ ] 1″ conduit minimum — ¾″ Sch 80 fails fill (Ch. 9 Tables 1, 4, 5).
  • [ ] EMT: supported within 3 ft of boxes and every 10 ft (358.30); outdoors, wet-rated compression fittings (358.42).
  • [ ] 1″ PVC: supported within 3 ft of boxes and every 3 ft (Table 352.30); bushings at ends (352.46).
  • [ ] Exterior PVC sunlight-resistant; Sch 80 where it could be hit (352.10(F), 352.12©, 300.4).
  • [ ] Expansion fitting on nearly every exterior PVC run — ¼″ movement at ~4 ft in Bend’s temperature swing (352.44).
  • [ ] ≤ 360° of bends between pull points (352.26 / 358.26).
  • [ ] Raceway complete before pulling wire (300.18).
  • [ ] Seal where raceway passes interior ↔ exterior, with sealant OK for the insulation; seal spare raceways (300.7(A); 2026: 300.9(A); OR 300.7(G)).
  • [ ] No unsupported span between house and sauna.

5. Exterior subpanel (sauna disconnect) #

  • [ ] NEMA 3R, listed outdoor, rated ≥ 50 A.
  • [ ] Main breaker or ≤ 6 breakers grouped here = building disconnect (225.31, 225.33, 225.34).
  • [ ] Readily accessible, nearest where the feeder enters (225.31(B); OR 2026 keeps “nearest” inside or out).
  • [ ] Bonding screw/strap removed; neutral isolated (250.32(B)(1), 250.24(A)(5), 250.142(B)).
  • [ ] Separate ground bar; feeder EGC and GEC land on it (408.40, 250.32(A)).
  • [ ] IBT or exposed, supported #6 bare Cu outside the panel (OR 250.94(A)).
  • [ ] Working space: 36″ deep × 30″ wide (or panel width) × 6½ ft; door opens 90° (110.26(A)).
  • [ ] Working space clear of the sauna door swing, steps, plantings.
  • [ ] Dedicated space above the panel, grade to 6 ft above, kept clear (110.26(E)(2)).
  • [ ] Top breaker handle ≤ 6 ft 7 in (240.24(A)).
  • [ ] ¼″ air space behind the enclosure (312.2).
  • [ ] Top entries use wet-location hubs; bottom/side preferred (312.2).
  • [ ] Only breakers listed for this panel; one neutral per terminal (110.3(B), 408.41).
  • [ ] Breakers: 45 A 2-pole heater; 15 A (#14) or 20 A (#12) lights (240.4(D)).
  • [ ] Heater breaker has a permanently installed lock-off (422.31(B), 110.25).
  • [ ] Lugs torqued; directory filled out; marked “Sauna disconnect” (110.14(D), 408.4(A), 110.22(A)).

6. Grounding electrode (at the sauna) #

  • [ ] Required — building is fed by a feeder (250.32(A)).
  • [ ] Two 8 ft, 5/8″ rods ≥ 6 ft apart (250.52(A)(5), 250.53(A)(2), (A)(3)).
  • [ ] Single rod only with a written ≤ 25 Ω test (OR 250.53(A)(2) Exc. 1) — plan on two in Bend soil.
  • [ ] Full 8 ft in soil; tops flush/below grade or clamps protected (250.53(A)(4), 250.10).
  • [ ] If rock: drive ≤ 45° or lay in a trench ≥ 30″ deep (250.53(A)(4)).
  • [ ] Clamps listed for direct burial; one conductor per clamp unless listed (250.70).
  • [ ] #6 Cu GEC, unspliced or irreversibly spliced (250.64©, 250.66(A)).
  • [ ] Rod-to-rod jumper #6 Cu (250.53©).
  • [ ] Exposed GEC protected or secured tight to the building (250.64(B)).
  • [ ] GEC on the ground bar, never the neutral.
  • [ ] Rods and clamps left visible for the cover inspection.

7. Heater circuit (subpanel → heater) #

  • [ ] Route low (4–6″ off floor), not across the header — keep out of heater radiation (Mfr).
  • [ ] #6 Cu recommended; #8 only with all-75 °C terminals and a cool route (110.14©, Table 310.15(B)(1)(1)).
  • [ ] If using a Xenio CX45: supply ≥ #6; CX45 → heater #8, 90 °C (Mfr).
  • [ ] Copper rated 90 °C (194 °F) inside sauna walls (Mfr).
  • [ ] No NM anywhere in the sauna walls or ceiling (334.80, 334.12(B)).
  • [ ] Singles in raceway (300.3(A)), or 90 °C MC.
  • [ ] Holes ≥ 1¼″ from edge or nail plates (300.4; 2026: 300.6).
  • [ ] Liquidtight flex to the heater’s box, entering 4–6″ above floor, 3 ft slack, 90 °C connector (Mfr).
  • [ ] Flex rated for the temperature (350.12) — standard jackets are often 60 °C.
  • [ ] Flex strapped within 12″ and every 4½ ft (350.30).
  • [ ] #10 Cu ground wire in the flex — flex can’t be the ground above 20 A (350.60, 250.118).
  • [ ] No plugs or pigtails (Mfr).
  • [ ] No GFCI — OR 210.8(F) covers outdoor receptacles only; Mfr forbids GFI.
  • [ ] Neutral pulled to the power unit (Xenio is “240 V 1N~”).

8. Heater install #

  • [ ] Heater listed to UL 875 (ORSC M1902.2).
  • [ ] Listed pairing: 8 kW on one supply cable → Xenio CX45 (CX30 max 7 kW) (Mfr).
  • [ ] Only listed controls — no DIY controller (110.3(B), ORSC M1902.2).
  • [ ] Room within range: KIP-80 250–425 ft³; Virta/Cilindro 8 kW up to 431 ft³, ceiling ≤ 7′6″ (Mfr).
  • [ ] Clearances: KIP-80 5″ to walls; Virta 2″ to walls, 42″ top-to-ceiling; min room height 75″ (Mfr).
  • [ ] Wood guard around heater (ORSC M1902.1); ≥ 2″ from heater, no taller than heater (Mfr).
  • [ ] Heat-resistant floor under heater — concrete/tile/heavy vinyl; no carpet (Mfr).
  • [ ] Mounting: KIP on 2×4 backing if wall < ¾″; Cilindro bolted to floor with its kit (Mfr).
  • [ ] Power unit dry, not outdoors, outside the hot room, 32–104 °F (Mfr).
  • [ ] In-room control panel (if any) ≥ 50 cm from heater, ≤ 1 m above floor (Mfr).
  • [ ] Sensor on heater centerline, ~4″ (100 mm) below ceiling (Mfr); within 6″ if separate (ORSC M1902.4).
  • [ ] Sensor ≥ 1 m from a 360° vent, ≥ 0.5 m from a directional one; supply air never blows on it (Mfr).
  • [ ] Correct sensor model (WX232/WX248 per manual); heater grade set in the control (Mfr).
  • [ ] Room limited to 194 °F (ORSC M1902.4); 1-hour max timer works (Mfr/listing).
  • [ ] Remote start only with a heater safety switch (Harvia SFE) and 6 h lockout (Mfr).
  • [ ] CAUTION placard inside above heater; WARNING placard outside on the door (Mfr).
  • [ ] Door opens outward, no lock (Mfr).
  • [ ] One heater per room (Mfr).
  • [ ] Manual left on site for the final.

9. Lights + exhaust fan circuit #

  • [ ] Own 15 A (#14) or 20 A (#12) breaker, or the Xenio light relay per its manual (210.23, 240.4(D)).
  • [ ] Xenio relay: 100 W max each, 120 V, needs neutral (Mfr).
  • [ ] At least one wall-switched light in the building (210.70).
  • [ ] Hot-room light listed, damp-rated, vapor-proof, wall-mounted, flush box (410.6, 410.10(A); Mfr).
  • [ ] Light ≥ 6″ below ceiling, not over heater or upper bench, ≥ 12″ from heater; under-bench ideal (Mfr).
  • [ ] Bulb ≤ the fixture’s marked max (KIP: 75 W) (Mfr).
  • [ ] Fixture supply wire rated as marked on the luminaire; 90 °C min (410.52).
  • [ ] Fan outside the hot room (inline or exterior wall), listed for location, ducted outdoors (Mfr, 110.3(B)).
  • [ ] Switches outside the hot room; exterior switch in a weatherproof cover (Mfr, 404.4(A)).
  • [ ] No receptacles inside the sauna room (Mfr).
  • [ ] GFCI not required on lights/fan (OR 210.8(F) Exc. 1).
  • [ ] AFCI not required — accessory building isn’t a 210.12(B) location; confirm with AHJ.

10. Receptacle #

  • [ ] At least one receptacle required at the building (210.52(G)(2)).
  • [ ] Never inside the hot room (Mfr). Get AHJ OK on an exterior one, in writing.
  • [ ] GFCI (OR 210.8(F)); GFCI device readily accessible.
  • [ ] Weather-resistant and tamper-resistant (406.9(B)(1), 406.12; 2026: 406.26).
  • [ ] Extra-duty in-use cover (406.9(B)(1)).
  • [ ] Wet-location box.
  • [ ] May share the lights circuit (210.23(A)).

11. Inspections #

  • [ ] Permit posted near the main panel (OAR 918-309-0000).
  • [ ] Request each inspection within 24 hours of work being ready (OAR 918-305-0430(1)).
  • [ ] Request via Bend’s CityView portal; note access (house panel, crawlspace) and a phone number.
  • [ ] Cover/rough-in: house and crawlspace feeder, boxes, subpanel, rods, and sauna wall wiring — before insulation, foil, or paneling.
  • [ ] Don’t cover until the inspection passes (OAR 918-305-0430(5)).
  • [ ] Fix corrections and request re-inspection within 20 days (OAR 918-305-0440).
  • [ ] Final: heater, lights, fan, labels, covers, manual on site. Portal won’t book it until all others pass.
  • [ ] Permit void if work doesn’t start, or stops, for 180 days (OAR 918-309-0000).
