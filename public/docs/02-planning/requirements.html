Overview #

The concrete requirements the finished sauna must meet. These translate the charter’s goals into checkable criteria that the design must satisfy.

Functional requirements #

  • [ ] Comfortably seats N people (TBD — drives size & heater kW).
  • [ ] Reaches and holds a proper bathing temperature (target range TBD).
  • [ ] Supports löyly (water on stones) — heater and ventilation must allow it.
  • [ ] Usable in the local climate year-round (or defined season).

Site & structural requirements #

  • [ ] Freestanding outdoor structure with a durable, weather-resistant envelope.
  • [ ] Foundation appropriate to soil, drainage, and frost/snow load.
  • [ ] Safe clearances around the heater per manufacturer + code.

Electrical requirements #

  • [ ] Dedicated circuit sized to the chosen heater.
  • [ ] Code-compliant (GFCI/disconnect as required locally).
  • [ ] Wiring rated for the sauna’s heat/humidity where applicable.

Non-functional / quality #

  • [ ] Buildable by a DIYer with common tools.
  • [ ] Maintainable (replaceable stones, cleanable interior, ventable moisture).
  • [ ] Within budget (see budget.md).

Constraints to confirm #

  • [ ] Occupancy target
  • [ ] Available electrical service (amps/voltage)
  • [ ] Budget ceiling
  • [ ] Local permit/code requirements (see permits-and-code.md)
