## Purpose

Define what we are building, the constraints, and what "done" looks like — so every
downstream research and design decision has a clear north star.

## The build in one line

A freestanding, outdoor, electric-heater sauna, constructed by the owner (DIY),
documented thoroughly enough that the build can be executed step by step.

## Goals

- A safe, code-compliant, comfortable sauna that reaches proper Finnish-style heat.
- A build achievable by a capable DIYer with common tools.
- Durable against the outdoor climate for the site.

## Definition of done

- [ ] Research digested and decisions justified.
- [ ] Complete design: dimensions, foundation, framing, insulation, electrical, ventilation, interior.
- [ ] A comprehensive, ordered build guide.
- [ ] Materials + tools list and budget.
- [ ] Code/permit and safety considerations documented.
