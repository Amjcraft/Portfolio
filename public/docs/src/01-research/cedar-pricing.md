## Overview

Rough 2026 Western Red Cedar (WRC) material pricing for the Pacific Northwest.
**Location: Bend, OR**, with supplier access to **Portland**. WRC grows in the PNW, so
regional pricing is relatively favorable — but **grade is the dominant cost lever**.
Prices are **materials only**, subject to market swings; confirm with local suppliers/mills.
_(This sheet was written when cedar was the whole-interior candidate; cedar is now the
bench-slat wood only — the pricing/grade notes still apply to that use.)_

## The key variable: grade

For a sauna, grade matters both for cost *and* function:

- **Clear, vertical-grain (VG) WRC** — no knots, stable, no sap bleed under heat,
  comfortable to touch. The standard for **benches** and ideal for hot-zone surfaces.
  **Most expensive.**
- **Knotty / STK ("Select Tight Knot") WRC** — attractive, much cheaper, fine for
  **walls, ceiling, and exterior siding**. Caveat: knots can bleed sap and get hot,
  so it's less ideal directly above the heater or where skin contacts it.

> **Strategy to control cost:** clear VG for **benches** (and optionally the hot
> upper wall/ceiling zone); knotty/STK for the rest of the walls, ceiling, and
> exterior siding. Going clear *everywhere* roughly doubles the interior wood cost.

## Planning ranges used in the estimate

- **Interior T&G (walls + ceiling):** **$6–16 / ft²** (knotty → clear VG span).
- **Exterior siding:** **$3.5–7 / ft²** (knotty → clear).
- **Bench slats (clear VG 2×4×8):** **~$30–48 / board**.

## Sourcing notes (Bend / PNW)

- Bend has local lumberyards; **Portland** widens access to cedar specialty mills and
  better clear-VG selection/pricing — worth a drive/delivery for the clear bench stock.
- Specialty **sauna suppliers** (Superior Saunas, West Coast Saunas, etc.) sell
  sauna-graded VG cedar but at a premium vs. a good local mill.
- Buy **clear VG** for benches from a mill that can pull vertical grain; buy
  **knotty/STK** locally for walls/ceiling/siding to save.
