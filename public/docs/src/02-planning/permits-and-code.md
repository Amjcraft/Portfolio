## Overview

Outdoor structures and their electrical work are often regulated. This captures
what applies locally so the build stays legal and safe. **Verify everything with
the local authority having jurisdiction (AHJ) — this doc is a checklist, not legal advice.**

## To confirm with the local building department

- [ ] Is a building permit required for a freestanding accessory structure this size?
- [ ] Setback requirements from property lines / other structures.
- [ ] Maximum size/height without a permit (if any exemption applies).
- [ ] Foundation requirements (frost depth, anchoring).
- [ ] Electrical permit + inspection requirements.
- [ ] Whether final electrical connection must be done by a licensed electrician.

## Electrical code touchpoints (verify locally)

- Dedicated circuit sized to heater.
- GFCI protection where required.
- Disconnecting means / appropriate wiring methods for a wet, hot environment.

## Safety-critical items (call these out in the build guide)

- Heater-to-combustible **clearances** per manufacturer + code.
- Proper **ventilation** to avoid CO/oxygen issues and manage moisture (less of a
  CO concern with electric vs. wood, but airflow still matters).
- Non-combustible protection under/around the heater as specified.

## Notes

Record the AHJ contact, permit numbers, and inspection dates here as they happen.
