## Overview

Rolling cost estimate for the ~**9×8 ft** freestanding wood sauna built on a **raised
2×6 floor over the existing** slab (no concrete work; drain runs in the floor cavity
to a French drain). Line-item quantities live in `materials-and-tools.md`; this page
is the roll-up and the target.

> **Accuracy:** Rough **2026 PNW** ranges (Bend/Portland), DIY labor assumed free.
> Wood is **Western Red Cedar throughout**, so the dominant lever is now **cedar
> grade** (clear VG vs. knotty). Electrical + heater are **deferred** and shown
> separately (heater ~$1,200). Cedar pricing detail in
> `../01-research/cedar-pricing.md`. Planning estimate, not a quote.

## All-in project ballpark

| | Low | High |
|---|---:|---:|
| Shell + interior | ~$6,500 | ~$13,000 |
| Electrical + heater (deferred) | ~$1,650 | ~$3,150 |
| **Total project** | **~$8,150** | **~$16,150** |

**Where you'll actually land:** with **hemlock** walls/ceiling, **cedar benches
(seat + upper apron only)**, cheap siding, DIY door, and no rear window, expect the
low–middle — roughly **$8,800–$10,800 all-in**. Hemlock grade + cedar bench quantity
are the main interior swings. Adding permeable sheathing (#3) is another ~$310–390.

> **Further savings:** the last big lever is the interior cedar itself — see
> [Cost Options](cost-options.html). Swapping walls/ceiling to **aspen** (keeping cedar
> as benches or an accent) would cut another ~$1,000–3,000. Tile flooring is *not* a saver.

## Where the money goes (biggest levers)

1. **Interior hemlock cladding** ($1.5k–3.1k) — biggest single line; ~305 ft² of walls/ceiling.
2. **Insulation + foil barrier (R-23)** ($710–1.1k) — largely fixed; don't cheap out here.
3. **Bench slats (clear VG cedar, seat + upper apron)** ($810–1.3k) — the one cedar splurge.
4. **Exterior siding (non-cedar)** ($459–1,377) — T1-11 (cheap) → LP SmartSide (durable).
5. **DIY door** ($180–420) — DIY saves ~$300–580 vs. prehung; no rear window now.

## Notes

- Built on a **raised 2×6 floor over the existing** slab — no concrete work (see `foundation.md`).
- Drain runs in the ~5.5″ floor cavity → 2″ PVC → French drain at daylight.
- Verify local **graywater-discharge** code for the French drain.
- Call out any task needing a licensed pro (notably final electrical connection).
- Refine once cladding species and regional prices are chosen — that collapses
  most of the low/high spread.
