## Overview

The structural skeleton: base/floor, walls, and roof. Stud depth must accommodate
the chosen insulation; the roof must shed the site's snow/rain load. Values below
are **preliminary** and feed the takeoff in `02-planning/materials-and-tools.md`.

## Structure summary

- **Footprint:** ~**9.5 × 8 ft** exterior. Front/back walls at ~9.5 ft; the extra ~6″
  vs. the earlier 9 ft compensates for the deeper 2×6 walls so the door still fits
  beside the heater. Interior ~**8.2 × 6.7 ft**.
- **Walls:** **2×6 framing @ 16″ OC** (upgraded from 2×4 for the cold Bend climate and
  the ≥8×8 size — per the Sauna Construction Handbook). Low wall **8 ft**, high wall **~9.5 ft**.
- **Roof:** **shed / mono-pitch**, sloping up ~1.5 ft over the 8 ft depth
  (~2.25:12). Simplest DIY roof and matches the "slope up ~1.5 ft" spec.

> **Note (roof slope):** ~2.25:12 is a *low* slope. Fine for rain with the right
> roofing (standing-seam metal or low-slope-rated membrane/shingle), but if the site
> sees real snow load, revisit pitch and rafter sizing. Confirm once climate is known.

## Base / floor

**Raised wood floor on the existing slab** (shed-style). No concrete work; the
platform cavity carries the drain out to a French drain. Full detail in `foundation.md`.

- **PT 2×6 joists @ 16″ OC** on the slab → **~5.5″ cavity** for the drain run.
- Capillary break between PT and concrete; **¾″ ext/PT plywood subfloor**.
- Waterproof finish floor pitched to a **floor drain**; removable **duckboard** on top.
- Drain: **2″ PVC**, sloped ≥¼″/ft, through the cavity → out → **French drain**.
- Walls bear on the platform's PT rim/sill; platform anchored to the slab as needed.

## Walls

- **2×6 studs @ 16″ OC**, single bottom plate + double top plate.
- Stud depth (**5.5″**) sets the mineral-wool cavity → **R-23** — see `insulation-and-vapor-barrier.md`.
- Build walls flat, then raise (per handbook); brace for lateral stability.
- Corrosion-resistant fasteners throughout (humidity).

## Roof

- Shed rafters (2×6) @ 16″ OC spanning the 8 ft, bearing on the tall and short walls.
- Roof sheathing + underlayment + roofing; drip edge + flashing.
- Modest overhang (~6–12″) to protect the walls.
