## Overview

The existing concrete slab is the **pad**. On top of it we build a **raised wood
floor** (shed-style), which creates a cavity underneath for the **drain line** to
run out to a **French drain** in open ground. **No concrete work, and no tapping
into the slab** — the drain never enters the concrete.

> **Decision:** Freestanding wood structure on a **raised 2×6 floor platform** over
> the existing slab. The ~5.5″ platform cavity carries the drain pipe out from under
> the sauna; nothing is cut into or tapped through the slab.

## Raised floor platform

- **PT 2×6 joists @ 16″ OC** laid over the slab, with rim joists — the on-edge 2×6
  gives a **~5.5″ cavity** (within the 4–6″ target) for the drain run.
- **Capillary break** (sill gasket / poly strip) between PT joists and concrete so
  the wood isn't sitting wet on the slab.
- **¾″ exterior/PT plywood subfloor** over the joists.
- **Waterproof finish floor** (membrane + tile, or a seamless waterproof coating)
  pitched to the floor drain, with a removable **duckboard** on top (see
  `benches-and-interior.md`).

## Drainage path

Water flows: **floor drain → platform cavity → PVC pipe → out from under the
structure → French drain in open ground.**

- **Floor drain:** shower-style drain fitting set at the low point of the finished floor.
- **Pipe:** **2″ Schedule-40 PVC (DWV)**, solvent-welded, sloped **≥ ¼″ per foot**
  toward the outlet. Route it in a joist bay (or drilled through joists within
  allowable limits), kept sloped the whole way.
- **Trap/screen:** a simple trap or screen at the drain keeps pests and cold air out
  (no sewer-gas concern — this is not tied to a sewer).
- **French drain:** the pipe daylights past the footprint into a gravel-filled trench
  with **perforated pipe wrapped in filter fabric**, sloped away to disperse into the
  ground.

> **Warning:** Confirm **local code for graywater discharge**, and site the French
> drain clear of property lines and anything downhill you don't want draining onto.

## Wall attachment

- Walls bear on the raised floor's **PT rim/sill**, fastened to the platform.
- Anchor the platform to the slab as needed for the freestanding structure (wedge/
  epoxy anchors via hammer drill — drilling anchor holes only, no pour).
