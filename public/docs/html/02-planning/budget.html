Overview #

Rolling cost estimate for the ~9×8 ft freestanding wood sauna built on a raised 2×6 floor over the existing slab (no concrete work; drain runs in the floor cavity to a French drain). Line-item quantities live in materials-and-tools.md; this page is the roll-up and the target.

Accuracy: Rough 2026 PNW ranges (Bend/Portland), DIY labor assumed free. Wood is Western Red Cedar throughout, so the dominant lever is now cedar grade (clear VG vs. knotty). Electrical + heater are deferred and shown separately (heater ~$1,200). Cedar pricing detail in ../01-research/cedar-pricing.md. Planning estimate, not a quote.

Target #

  • Budget ceiling: TBD
  • Contingency: ~12% built into the totals below.

Roll-up by system (shell + interior) #

# System Low High
1 Raised floor + drainage (on existing slab) $553 $1,224
2 Wall framing (2×6) $406 $676
3 Roof framing $108 $192
4 Sheathing & weather barrier $265 $489
5 Exterior envelope (cheap non-cedar siding) $729 $2,007
6 Insulation & vapor barrier (R-23) $710 $1,090
7 Interior cladding (hemlock) & benches (cedar slats, hem-fir frame) $2,642 $5,010
8 Openings & ventilation (DIY door + vents; no window) $220 $520
9 Hardware & finishes $155 $370
Subtotal $5,788 $11,578
Contingency (~12%) $695 $1,389
Shell + interior total ~$6,500 ~$13,000

2×6 walls added here are ~cost-neutral — framing + R-23 insulation went up, but the thicker walls shrank the interior, cutting the cedar cladding by about the same. Permeable sheathing (#3) is not in these numbers; see Cost Options (+$310–390).

Deferred (electrical & heater) #

Item Low High
Heater (~9.5 kW) + stones $1,200 $1,200
Controller / circuit materials $150 $750
Electrician (if required) $300 $1,200
Deferred subtotal ~$1,650 ~$3,150

All-in project ballpark #

Low High
Shell + interior ~$6,500 ~$13,000
Electrical + heater (deferred) ~$1,650 ~$3,150
Total project ~$8,150 ~$16,150

Where you’ll actually land: with hemlock walls/ceiling, cedar benches (seat + upper apron only), cheap siding, DIY door, and no rear window, expect the low–middle — roughly $8,800–$10,800 all-in. Hemlock grade + cedar bench quantity are the main interior swings. Adding permeable sheathing (#3) is another ~$310–390.

Further savings: the last big lever is the interior cedar itself — see Cost Options. Swapping walls/ceiling to aspen (keeping cedar as benches or an accent) would cut another ~$1,000–3,000. Tile flooring is not a saver.

Where the money goes (biggest levers) #

  1. Interior hemlock cladding ($1.5k–3.1k) — biggest single line; ~305 ft² of walls/ceiling.
  2. Insulation + foil barrier (R-23) ($710–1.1k) — largely fixed; don’t cheap out here.
  3. Bench slats (clear VG cedar, seat + upper apron) ($810–1.3k) — the one cedar splurge.
  4. Exterior siding (non-cedar) ($459–1,377) — T1-11 (cheap) → LP SmartSide (durable).
  5. DIY door ($180–420) — DIY saves ~$300–580 vs. prehung; no rear window now.

Notes #

  • Built on a raised 2×6 floor over the existing slab — no concrete work (see foundation.md).
  • Drain runs in the ~5.5″ floor cavity → 2″ PVC → French drain at daylight.
  • Verify local graywater-discharge code for the French drain.
  • Call out any task needing a licensed pro (notably final electrical connection).
  • Refine once cladding species and regional prices are chosen — that collapses most of the low/high spread.
