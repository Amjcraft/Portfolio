Purpose #

Define what we are building, the constraints, and what “done” looks like — so every downstream research and design decision has a clear north star.

The build in one line #

A freestanding, outdoor, electric-heater sauna, constructed by the owner (DIY), documented thoroughly enough that the build can be executed step by step.

Goals #

  • A safe, code-compliant, comfortable sauna that reaches proper Finnish-style heat.
  • A build achievable by a capable DIYer with common tools.
  • Durable against the outdoor climate for the site.

Known constraints & unknowns #

Topic Status Notes
Heat source Decided Electric heater (kiuas).
Location Decided Outdoor, freestanding.
Builder Decided DIY.
Interior capacity (persons) Open Drives dimensions + heater kW.
Footprint / dimensions Preliminary Exterior ~8×8 ft (8.5 max one side). See 03-design/dimensions-and-layout.md.
Heater Preliminary ~9.5 kW electric, 20×24″ element. See 03-design/electrical-and-heater.md.
Budget ceiling Open See 02-planning/budget.md.
Local climate / snow load Open Affects foundation + framing.
Electrical service available Known 50 A line available — fits 9.5 kW only at 240 V; confirm voltage.

Definition of done #

  • [ ] Research digested and decisions justified.
  • [ ] Complete design: dimensions, foundation, framing, insulation, electrical, ventilation, interior.
  • [ ] A comprehensive, ordered build guide.
  • [ ] Materials + tools list and budget.
  • [ ] Code/permit and safety considerations documented.

Open questions #

  • [ ] How many people should it comfortably seat?
  • [x] What’s the electrical service situation at the site? — 50 A line available.
  • [ ] Confirm heater voltage (240 V vs. 208 V) — determines if 9.5 kW fits the 50 A circuit.
  • [ ] Target budget range?
