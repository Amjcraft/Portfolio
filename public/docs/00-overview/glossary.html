Overview #

Shared vocabulary so the docs stay precise. Add terms as they come up.

Terms #

Term Meaning
Sauna A heated room for dry/steam heat bathing; here, Finnish-style.
Kiuas The sauna heater/stove. Ours is electric.
Löyly The burst of steam produced when water is thrown on the heated stones.
Sauna stones Heat-retaining rocks (e.g. peridotite/olivine) atop the heater that produce löyly.
Vapor barrier Foil membrane behind the cladding that blocks moisture from entering the wall assembly. Critical in saunas.
Cladding The interior wood surface (commonly aspen, alder, cedar, or thermally-modified wood).
Duckboard Removable slatted floor grating for drainage/comfort.
Foil / radiant barrier Reflective aluminum layer reflecting heat back into the room and stopping vapor.
Air intake / exhaust The two vents that drive sauna air circulation (mechanical or gravity).
kW rating Heater power; sized to the cabin’s air volume (plus adjustments for glass/uninsulated area).
Clearance Required minimum distance between the heater and combustible surfaces/benches.
Thermally-modified wood Heat-treated wood with improved moisture stability and lower resin — popular for outdoor sauna cladding.
